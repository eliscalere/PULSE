SOP 01 / PROCEDURE



Update the source record, not the summary.
 01
          Open Projects, locate or create the project, and confirm the project identity before editing.

 02
          In Settings, maintain lifecycle, technical health, ownership, dates, portfolios, program, and end-item configuration as applicable.

 03
          In People, maintain the roster and responsibilities so ownership remains current.

 04
          In Tracker, create or update work items with owner, start date, due date, workflow value, health, notes, and related documents.

 05
          Use Blocked only when a dependency prevents progress; record the dependency and the recovery action. Use On Hold only with a reason and restart condition.

 06
          Use Gantt to confirm schedule placement. Use Boards only for the board workflow; do not assume board activity updated Tracker items.

 07
          Confirm the save indication, refresh the record, and review the Activity Log when required.

SOP 05 / PROCEDURE



Never overwrite the evidence trail.
 01
          Open Document Review and select New Review.

 02
          Record the project, type, title, due date, revision label, current file, reviewers, and required signers.

 03
          Reviewers open the assigned review record, assess the current revision, and record a decision with actionable comments.

 04
          When changes are requested, upload a new revision and preserve the prior revision history. Run the review again for the new revision.

 05
          When all required reviewers approve, follow the displayed workflow for review completion or final-pack signing.

 06
          Archive only through the workflow after required review and signature activity is complete.

 07
          Refresh the record and verify reviewer decisions, revision history, and workflow state.



    EXPECTED RESULT                                                                                           ESCALATE

    The record shows who reviewed which revision, what decision was made,                                     Missing reviewer state, incorrect revision reset, inaccessible signer
    and the current workflow state.                                                                           control, or unexpected archive behavior.

  Completion/signature                          Required completion or signing activity according to the displayed path.


  Audit trail                                   Change history sufficient to reconstruct the workflow.




      Records: review record, revision files, decisions, comments, signature activity, notifications, and audit history.

Export procedure

 01
         Verify the source records and the access required for the output.

 02
         Generate the export through the current PULSE workflow.

 03

 04

Fault response procedure

 01
         Stop repeated retries when the fault could alter data or obscure evidence.

 02
         Capture time, route, user role, record identifier, action, expected result, actual result, browser or device, and approved screenshot.

 03
         Check diagnostics and Activity Log when authorized.

 04
         File or update the support ticket with factual evidence.

 05
         Maintainers validate locally, then on a safe SharePoint site, then on the actual hosted page.


      Deployment regression: restore the prior known-good package, preserve the failed artifact and evidence, and isolate the source change before trying a new release.
