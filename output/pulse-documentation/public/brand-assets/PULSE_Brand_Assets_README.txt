PULSE BRAND ASSET PACKAGE - VERSION 1.3
========================================

New in version 1.3
------------------
The Secondary Lockup combines the two-dot mark with a clean PULSE wordmark that contains no internal dots.
Use it on splash screens, high-visibility horizontal signatures, and selected cover treatments.

Core rules
----------
- Keep the dot pair on its original upper-left to lower-right diagonal.
- Maintain a gap of approximately 1.5D to 1.75D between the dot pair and wordmark.
- The secondary PULSE wordmark must not contain dots above U or below S.
- Keep at least 1D clear space around the complete lockup.
- Use only black or white identity artwork.
- Do not recolor, rotate, stretch, or substitute the primary dotted wordmark inside the secondary lockup.

See the included brand guide for complete usage, sizing, color, and typography rules.
