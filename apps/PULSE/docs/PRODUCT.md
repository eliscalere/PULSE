# Product

## Register

product

## Users
AEWTTR IPT personnel and site members use this app inside SharePoint / Firepit to manage projects, travel requests, document reviews, meetings, and internal coordination. They are usually working in an authenticated operational workflow and need reliable task-focused tools rather than marketing-style experiences.

## Product Purpose
PULSE is an internal SharePoint-backed operations workspace for AEWTTR. It helps the team capture, review, and track operational records in one place, with SharePoint Lists and SharePoint files serving as the system of record.

## Brand Personality
Practical, trustworthy, structured.

## Anti-references
This should not feel like a consumer SaaS landing page, a playful startup dashboard, or an experimental design concept. Avoid decorative UI that gets in the way of task completion, and avoid exports that look informal when they need to resemble official forms.

## Design Principles
- Preserve earned familiarity so users trust the interface immediately.
- Keep SharePoint-backed workflows clear, inspectable, and dependable.
- Prioritize task completion over visual novelty.
- Make official-looking outputs feel structured and printable.
- Use visual polish to support clarity, not to add noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for readable, high-contrast interfaces, clear labels, and reduced reliance on color alone for state. Exported forms should remain legible when printed or opened in constrained environments.
