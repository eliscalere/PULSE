# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SharePoint homepage visitors checking team travel and leave at a glance, then opening a request only when they need more context.

## Product Purpose

Provide a compact, readable monthly availability calendar that fits directly into a SharePoint homepage web part.

## Brand Personality

Operational, clear, dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized dashboard cards, decorative visual effects, and nested scroll areas that conceal calendar context.

## Design Principles

- Keep the month visible at one glance.
- Use refiners to reduce visual noise without hiding the calendar controls.
- Make event color and calendar placement carry the primary meaning.
- Reveal detail only after an intentional click.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text contrast, text labels alongside color, keyboard-accessible controls, and no motion required to understand calendar state.
